## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory depth and timeless character of Cambodia: the sun-warmed sandstone of Angkorian monuments, rich Mekong waters, deep jungle canopies, and the luster of raw Khmer silk. It balances deep cultural reverence with modern, exploratory adventure.

The target audience spans cultural travelers, eco-adventurers, and regional explorers seeking authenticity, clarity, and trust. The interface crafts an immersive editorial feel that balances grounded solidity with refined, hospitality-grade clarity.

### Design Movement: Organic Editorial Heritage
- **Tone:** Grounded, evocative, respectful, precise.
- **Visual Cadence:** Rich tactile backgrounds resembling unbleached mulberry paper paired with deep verdant and mineral accents. High legibility, dignified editorial typography, and generous negative space evoke a curated field journal or luxury travel periodical rather than a generic booking engine.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from Cambodia's landscape and architectural heritage.

### Light Mode (Default)
- **Background (`#FAF7F2`):** Warm unbleached paper tone; softens contrast and avoids digital glare.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp focal plane for cards, sheets, and interactive elements.
- **Border (`#E7E1D6`):** Sandstone-derived divider tone that defines edges without high visual noise.
- **Primary Deep Jungle Teal (`#0F5E59`):** Represents dense cardamom forest canopies. Used for primary CTAs, active indicators, and high-emphasis brand anchors.
- **Secondary Laterite Terracotta (`#C4552D`):** Rich earth and temple foundation stone. Used for focal highlights, tags, and adventure markers.
- **Accent Saffron Gold (`#E8A317`):** Sacred monastic fabrics and Mekong sunsets. Reserved for ratings, special recommendations, and celebratory actions.
- **Text Main (`#1C1B19`):** Deep charcoal-sepia; maintains high contrast without the stark harshness of pure black.
- **Text Muted (`#6B665C`):** Weathered river stone; applied to secondary metadata, captions, and structural guidelines.

### Dark Mode
- **Background (`#0B0F14`):** Night temple atmosphere.
- **Surface (`#131A22`):** Grounded river-basin charcoal.
- **Surface Elevated (`#1A2430`):** Floating card and dialog layer.
- **Border (`#263241`):** Muted midnight boundary.
- **Primary (`#2DD4BF`):** Luminous tropical patina teal for high legibility on dark grounds.
- **Accent (`#FBBF24`):** Illuminated lantern saffron.

### Semantic Status
- **Confirmed / Success:** `#15803D` (Verdant leaf)
- **Pending / In Progress:** `#D97706` (Amber resin)
- **Rejected / Destructive:** `#B91C1C` (Deep lacquer red)
- **Draft / Inactive:** `#64748B` (Slate silt)

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances literary sophistication with high-utility booking legibility.

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys editorial travel narrative, architectural scale, and authenticity. Used for destination titles, section anchors, and hero statements.
- **Interface & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern, clear geometric humanist grotesque. Provides clarity for prices, itineraries, schedules, and form fields.
- **Multilingual Khmer Support:** Fallback sequence must prioritize `Noto Sans Khmer` paired dynamically alongside Plus Jakarta Sans to maintain equivalent x-height, baseline alignment, and optical balance across Khmer script elements.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an adaptable 12-column layout on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a 4-column system on mobile devices.

### Grid & Margins
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, max-width 1280px, 48px (`margin`) page margins, 24px (`gutter`) column gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 or 12 columns, 32px (`margin-tablet`) margins, 20px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px (`margin-mobile`) margins, 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters.

### Spacing Strategy
Rhythm follows an 8pt architectural cadence with 4pt half-steps (`space-xs: 4px`, `space-sm: 8px`, `space-md: 16px`, `space-lg: 24px`, `space-xl: 40px`). Dense booking widgets and date matrices use compact `space-xs` and `space-sm` gaps, while itinerary story modules use generous `space-xl` layouts to encourage breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient diffusion rather than harsh synthetic dropshadows. Shadows take cues from natural sunlight passing through jungle foliage or temple corridors.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Used for background canvas and resting form controls. Defined by borders (`#E7E1D6` in light, `#263241` in dark) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Subdued surface distinction. `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 27, 25, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(28, 27, 25, 0.02)`. Combined with a 1px perimeter border.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Cards / Popovers):** Warm diffused lift. `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(28, 27, 25, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(28, 27, 25, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Sticky Booking Bars):** High-prominence suspension. `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(15, 94, 89, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(28, 27, 25, 0.06)`. Note the subtle warm teal tinting in the ambient spread.

### Glassmorphism Treatment
Floating navigation headers and contextual overlays employ a backdrop-blur of `12px` over an 85% opacity surface (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.85)` in light, `rgba(19, 26, 34, 0.85)` in dark), allowing landscape photography to peek through while maintaining text contrast.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances organic warmth with structural durability.

- **Cards & Major Panels:** Fixed at `16px` radius (`rounded-lg`), creating an approachable, tactile card contour that references rounded temple tiles and carved lintels.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Fixed at `10px` radius. This deliberate, tailored roundness feels structural, precise, and secure under touch or pointer interaction.
- **Tags, Badges, & Chips:** Strictly `9999px` (Pill-shaped), establishing an immediate visual distinction between actionable filters, status indicators, and content containers.
- **Media Containers:** Image hero frames incorporate the standard `16px` radius; thumbnail galleries within itinerary rows share matching corner curves to maintain geometric rhythm.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background Deep Jungle Teal (`#0F5E59`), label `#FFFFFF` in `label-lg`, radius `10px`. Hover shifts to `#0B4643` with elevation Level 1.
- **Secondary (Laterite):** Terracotta fill (`#C4552D`), label `#FFFFFF`. Used for direct conversions such as "Book Expedition" or "Check Dates".
- **Outlined / Neutral:** 1px border `#E7E1D6`, background transparent, text `#1C1B19`. Hover transitions surface to `#FFFFFF` with Level 1 shadow.
- **Padding:** 12px vertical by 20px horizontal for default height (44px target touch area).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Height & Form:** 44px height, `10px` radius, 1px border `#E7E1D6`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **States:** Active/focus yields a 1.5px outline in Primary Teal (`#0F5E59`) with a 3px soft tint ring (`rgba(15, 94, 89, 0.15)`). Muted placeholder text in `#6B665C`.
- **Search & Booking Filter Bar:** Unified multi-segment bar bound in a single `16px` container with vertical divider lines.

### Cards (Destinations & Tours)
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E7E1D6`, radius `16px`, Level 1 ambient shadow.
- **Visuals:** 16:10 aspect ratio image overflow-hidden at top; content area padded with `space-md` (16px).
- **Metadata Layout:** Editorial title in `Playfair Display` (`title-lg`), accompanied by Saffron Gold (`#E8A317`) rating stars, duration chips, and a crisp bottom row pinning price and CTA.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Pill shape (`9999px`), padding 4px 10px, typography `label-caps`.
  - *Confirmed:* Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`.
  - *Pending:* Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`.
  - *Heritage/Eco Marker:* Background `rgba(15, 94, 89, 0.08)`, text `#0F5E59`.
- **Filter Chips:** 32px height, pill shape, border 1px `#E7E1D6`, surface `#FAF7F2`. When selected, fill turns to `#0F5E59` with crisp white text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Dimensions:** 20px width and height. Checkbox has a `4px` corner radius; radio is fully circular.
- **Selected State:** Solid fill `#0F5E59` with clean white glyph. Border `#0F5E59`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Itinerary Timeline:** Vertical sandstone line (`#E7E1D6`, 2px width) connected by terracotta and teal node dots (8px circle with 4px white halo), anchoring daily stops, temple routes, and travel transfers.
- **Temple Respect & Heritage Notice Box:** Muted parchment container tinted with Saffron Gold (`rgba(232, 163, 23, 0.08)`), left accent border (3px solid `#E8A317`), containing advisory iconography for cultural dress codes and preservation rules.
